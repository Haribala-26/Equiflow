# EQUIFLOW — Problem Statement

## The Emergency Logistics Crisis

During humanitarian disasters (such as the Chennai floods), response organizations face three acute challenges:

1. **Information Asymmetry & Report Overload**: Emergency reports arrive via chaotic, unstructured channels (social media, voice notes, field reports) with missing details, inconsistencies, and varying urgencies.
2. **The "Equal vs. Fair" Fallacy**: Distributing equal packages across all wards results in wealthy areas receiving surplus food while inundated, vulnerable informal settlements run out of infant food and life-saving insulin within hours.
3. **Black-Box Skepticism**: First responders and community leaders reject algorithmic decisions if they cannot audit *why* a particular ward received priority over another.

---

## The EQUIFLOW Solution

EQUIFLOW replaces ad-hoc spreadsheets and black-box models with:
- An open, versioned 5-factor mathematical formula.
- A multi-tier allocation engine combining floor coverage and weighted proportional distribution.
- Real-time map synchronization and complete audit trails with mandatory rationale for any human override.
