# EQUIFLOW — Product Overview

## 1. Vision & Mission

**EQUIFLOW** is an AI-powered emergency resource distribution and decision-support platform designed to ensure fair, transparent, and mathematically sound allocation of critical supplies during disasters.

Equal distribution is often fundamentally unfair. When emergencies strike, different communities experience vastly different severities, infrastructure collapse, and demographic vulnerabilities. EQUIFLOW balances needs, urgency, vulnerability, and available stock to direct aid where the human impact is greatest.

---

## 2. Core Value Propositions

1. **Deterministic Fairness**: Transparent, explainable priority scoring eliminating subjective bias.
2. **Constrained Resource Optimization**: Algorithmic allocation ensuring minimum essential coverage while respecting finite inventory.
3. **Responsible AI Integration**: Google Gemini assists with unstructured emergency report extraction, summary generation, and plain-language explanations without ever unilaterally dictating allocation quantities.
4. **Elephant–Goldfish Review**: Adversarial fresh-eyes architectural and document verification to catch systemic blind spots.
5. **Real Geographic Grounding**: Full OpenStreetMap integration centered on Chennai, Tamil Nadu, synchronized in real time with all metrics.
6. **What-If Simulation & Shortage Forecasting**: Enabling coordinators to model stock reductions, population surges, and projected supply depletion.
